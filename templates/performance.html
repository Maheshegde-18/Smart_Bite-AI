Rigorous ML Pipeline & Methodology
1. Chronological Splitting Data is strictly split 80% train and 20% test chronologically by timestamp, mimicking real-world production forecasting without temporal lookahead bias.
2. Zero Data Leakage Preprocessing scalers (StandardScaler) are fitted solely on training data and then transformed across validation/test sets and live production inputs.
3. Dual Separate Models Two distinct instances of sklearn.linear_model.LinearRegression are trained separately so demand and waste dynamics are learned independently.
